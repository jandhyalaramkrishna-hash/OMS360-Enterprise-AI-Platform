Business failures (Failed Orders, Duplicate Orders, Payment Failures) are order-level problems a customer or support agent would recognize — a payment was rejected, an order didn't go through, the same order was submitted twice. Technical failures (Dead Letter Queue / DLQ) are backend messaging errors — Kafka events the system could not process after automatic retries. They are tracked separately because different teams act on them: support/ops on business failures, engineering on technical ones. A business failure is not the same thing as a DLQ entry, even when both relate to the same order.
